## Validation (proven 2026-07-31 via `scripts/profile/validate_p3.py`)

The validation harness builds **two fresh PostgreSQL 16 databases**, applies P3,
captures both, and checks:

1. **Self-consistent:** stored fingerprint == recomputed fingerprint (both builds). ✅
2. **Deterministic:** two captures produce identical fingerprint. ✅
3. **Matches golden:** both captures == committed `golden-manifest.json`. ✅
4. **Secret-scan clean:** no passwords, tokens, credentials, production IPs, or emails. ✅

This runs in CI (`Validate P3 fixture` step in the backend job).

## Byte-determinism

`generate_p3.py` is byte-deterministic: identical P1 input produces identical
`schema.sql` and `seed.sql` bytes (no embedded timestamps). Verified by
generating twice and comparing SHA-256.
